SRHR Project Advocacy Portal

Project Period: Counties Covered: 4 Last Updated:

The EACHRights SRHR Advocacy Knowledge Management Dashboard is a centralized platform that tracks advocacy progress on priority Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR) legal and policy initiatives across Homa Bay, Migori, Kilifi, and Kwale under the Amplify Change-funded project, "Taking on the Legal and Policy Advocacy Challenge: Promoting Synergies Among SRHR Social Movements in Kenya." Updated quarterly using data submitted by EACHRights Programme and MEL teams, the dashboard provides real-time insights into advocacy activities, policy and legislative milestones, implementation progress, stakeholder engagement, and county advocacy performance. It also serves as a repository of ready-to-print advocacy materials and knowledge products for use by EACHRights, partners, and government stakeholders to support coordinated, evidence-informed advocacy.

About This Portal

This portal is maintained by EACHRights (East African Centre for Human Rights) with support from Amplify Change. Data is updated by project field teams via the Admin panel. County policy documents can be uploaded and attached per county.

For questions or data corrections, contact the EACHRights monitoring and evaluation team.
